# Draft SOW — Nimbus Fitness CPQ Phase 1 Expansion

Doc type: DRAFT_SOW
Status: draft

## Scope
Auctor will configure Salesforce CPQ guided selling across Treadmills, Bikes, Rowers, and Accessories. The implementation will support North America and EMEA selling motions.

Auctor will configure quote templates for the North America and EMEA sales teams. Auctor will also include an SAP integration so order data is available to the finance team.

The solution will provide seamless CPQ performance for all sales users and a user-friendly admin experience.

## Deliverables
- Salesforce CPQ guided selling configuration for four product families.
- Discount approval workflow using a 10% approval threshold.
- Quote templates for North America and EMEA.
- Real-time two-way NetSuite synchronization.
- SAP order integration.
- Admin training for up to 12 Nimbus users.

## Dependencies
- NetSuite credentials must be provided before integration testing.
- Final legal language must be provided before quote template configuration.

## Change Requests
Small changes requested by Nimbus stakeholders may be handled during weekly check-ins without a formal signed change order.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Guided selling is fast and intuitive.
- CPQ supports all required selling motions.
- NetSuite sync works seamlessly.
