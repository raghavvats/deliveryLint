# Change Order — GlobalFoods WMS Enhancements

Doc type: CHANGE_ORDER
Status: draft

## Scope
This change order adds voice picking, labor management optimization, real-time SAP inventory updates, a shift scheduling dashboard, and support for the Dallas DC in addition to Chicago.

The work also includes procurement of new scanner headsets and configuration of transportation management integration so outbound shipments are easier to manage.

## Deliverables
- Voice picking workflow.
- Labor optimization recommendations.
- Real-time SAP inventory sync.
- Dallas DC rollout.
- Shift scheduling dashboard.
- New scanner headset procurement.
- Transportation management integration.

## Timeline
These enhancements will be completed during the current phase without impacting the approved go-live date.

## Responsibilities
Auctor will own all configuration, UAT execution, scanner hardware procurement, SAP basis support, and DC operations coordination.
GlobalFoods will provide feedback as needed.

## Assumptions
The enhancements can reuse existing WMS data and should be straightforward.

## Dependencies
- SAP access is needed.
- Warehouse users must be available.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Enhancements work seamlessly.
- Users can complete workflows quickly.
