# Signed SOW — GlobalFoods WMS Rollout Phase 1

Doc type: SIGNED_SOW
Origin: joint
Status: signed
Recency date: 2026-01-28

## Scope
- Configure Manhattan WMS receiving, putaway, picking, packing, and shipping workflows for the Chicago DC.
- Integrate Manhattan WMS with SAP ECC inventory postings through nightly batch files.
- Configure handheld scanner flows for receiving and picking.
- Train up to 30 warehouse supervisors and leads.

## Out of Scope
- Voice picking.
- Labor management optimization.
- Additional distribution centers beyond Chicago DC.
- Real-time SAP integration.
- Transportation management system integration.
- Custom hardware procurement.

## Baseline Dates
- Solution design complete: 2026-02-18
- Configuration complete: 2026-03-27
- UAT complete: 2026-04-17
- Go-live: 2026-04-29

## Responsibilities
- Auctor owns Manhattan WMS configuration and training materials.
- GlobalFoods owns scanner hardware, SAP file specs, DC operations availability, and UAT execution.
- SAP integration testing requires GlobalFoods SAP basis support.

## Change Control
All scope changes require a signed change order with scope, timeline impact, fee impact, owner responsibilities, assumptions, and dependencies.
