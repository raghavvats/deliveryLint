Doc type: SIGNED_SOW

## In Scope
- Build onboarding workflow for US full-time employees only.
- Integrate Workday employee profile data into ServiceNow HR Service Delivery.
- Create three onboarding task templates: IT equipment, payroll setup, and benefits enrollment.
- Send onboarding reminder emails from ServiceNow.
- Provide HR admin training for 6 users.

## Out of Scope
- Contractor onboarding.
- International onboarding.
- Slack provisioning or Slack workflow automation.
- Background check vendor integration.
- Mobile app development.
